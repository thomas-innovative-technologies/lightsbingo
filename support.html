Common questions

How do I play with friends on other phones? Each phone starts its own game. Pick one game code and have everyone type it on the New game screen, with the same holiday and difficulty. Everyone still gets their own card.

How do I check someone's Bingo? Start a game with their code, their name spelled the same way, and the same holiday and difficulty. You'll see their exact card.

What is Tonight's card? Everyone who plays that night gets the same card on any phone, so you can race another car or compare with family across town. A new card every night.

Why don't I hear music? Turn on Theme music in Settings. Sounds follow your iPhone's silent switch and volume, and play alongside your car's radio.

Where are my scores in Game Center? Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings, then turn on Game Center in the app's Settings and choose whose scores this phone reports.

Can I delete my data? Everything is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it all, including photos you took in the app. Game Center scores are managed by Apple in your iPhone's Game Center settings.
