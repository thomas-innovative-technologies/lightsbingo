Lights Bingo

by Thomas Innovative Technologies

Spot holiday decorations from the back seat and race your family to Bingo. Every player gets their own card of outdoor decorations to find, from inflatable snowmen to the elusive narwhal. Rare finds glow red.

Five holidays: Christmas, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Valentine's Day and the Fourth of July.

Play your way: classic 5×5 cards, a 3×3 kids card, or Tonight's card, the same card for everyone that night.

Season fun: family points leaderboard, badges, Game Center leaderboards and achievements.

Passengers play. The driver drives.
